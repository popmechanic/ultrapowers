**Grammar:** claims-v1

**Claim:** When I read a run's record, every fold either made a queued task ready, closed the run, or adopted work that had waited a full suite's length — a plan with no edges folds once, and a task lands on the hub as landed, then adopted, never as needing a human when it doesn't. (elicited)

### Task 1: A lane folds only when the fold releases a queued task, ends the run, or adopts a result that has aged a suite's length

**Claim:** When I read a run's record, every fold either made a queued task ready, closed the run, or adopted work that had waited a full suite's length — and a plan with no edges folds once. (derived)
Machine: M1. A lane that frees claims an epoch only when at least one of three holds at that instant, and the `driver:wave-adopted` (or `driver:wave-blocked`) event it produces carries `why` naming which: `released` — some task not yet dispatched, not failed and not downstream of a failed or blocked task, that is not ready now, has every predecessor an edge names either adopted or among the pending results, and the event also carries `released: [<those task ids>]` in plan order; `end` — after this landing no worker is in flight, no fold is running, no task is ready and nothing is pending but these results; `aged` — the oldest pending result has waited `foldAgeMs` milliseconds or more since it landed (so `0` holds at the landing itself). M2. `foldAgeMs` is read from the run's arguments when it is a non-negative number; otherwise it is the larger of `60000` and the wall, in milliseconds, of the baseline suite the run measured (the age clause is off until the baseline has settled); with `foldAgeMs: 0` every landing folds at its own instant, which is the BASE rule. M3. A run of three tasks with no edges, whose implementers land at three different times with `foldAgeMs` large, adopts all three in exactly one epoch with `why: 'end'`; the same run with `foldAgeMs: 0` adopts them in more than one epoch, the first `why: 'aged'`. M4. A run of tasks A, B, C with edges A→C and B→C, A landing first, adopts A and B together in one epoch with `why: 'released'` and `released: ['C']` only once B has landed, C is dispatched after that adoption on its head, and C's own landing is adopted in a second epoch with `why: 'end'`; a run where A lands, then nothing else lands for longer than a small `foldAgeMs` while B is still running, adopts A alone with `why: 'aged'`. M5. A lane waiting with nothing ready and results pending wakes on its own when the oldest pending result reaches `foldAgeMs`, without another landing. M6. `fleet/CONTRACT.md`'s evidence bullet states the three triggers and `foldAgeMs` with its default and its `0` reading, and still carries, in this order, the words `epoch`, `ready`, `slot frees`, `driver:wave-adopted` and `descendant`. M7. `fleet/tests/test_run_engine_ready_set.mjs`, `fleet/tests/test_run_engine_joined_proofs.mjs` and `fleet/tests/test_run_engine_proof_runs.mjs` — all three edited by this task — pass on the patched tree: the first's header M2 text states the new rule, its scenario `c1` is re-aimed to one epoch of `['X','Y','Z']` with `why: 'end'`, and every other scenario of that sim (`a1`, `a2`, `b1`, `b2`, `d1`, `d2`, `e1`, `e2`), whose subject is not the fold trigger, passes `foldAgeMs: 0` to the engine so its BASE expectations hold unchanged; the other two sims pass `foldAgeMs: 0` in their rigs for the same reason; and every other sim under `fleet/tests/` passes through the bridge on the patched tree. M8. With `foldAgeMs: 0`, a result that lands while a fold is running is adopted by the next epoch and never by the one in flight: three no-edge tasks X, Y, Z where Y's and Z's implementers return only after the kernel's first `fold` has begun yield exactly two adoptions, the first `tasks` `['X']` and the second `tasks` `['Y','Z']` — the pin scenario `c1` carried at BASE, kept in the new exam.
