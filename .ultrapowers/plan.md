# Three record-only Jev rows: a second opinion beside every blocking finding, a difficulty read beside the tier, and whose-red-is-it on every red suite

**Grammar:** claims-v1

**Claim:** Each is one `POST /v1/systemone` from the driver, appended to `events.jsonl` under a kind of its own, mirrored to the kata run issue like every driver row, and read by nothing in this ticket. (quoted from #1096)
**Summary:** Three questions about a calibrated second reader cannot be answered from the tags because the input it needs only exists while a run is live. This plan has the driver record one snap judgment beside every blocking finding, every task's tier and every red suite, and act on none of it. After five runs the record says whether a second opinion, a difficulty read or a whose-red-is-it read is worth wiring in; the rollback is deleting the three appends.

**Goal:** #1096 as an `experiment`: the driver puts one question set to Jev (TypeSafe's System One model, reached as the `typesafe` http-proxy at the exe.dev edge) at three sites of `fleet/run-engine.mjs` — beside every reviewer-returned blocking finding, beside every task's tier at dispatch and at review, and in the unattributed-red branch of a fold — and appends what came back as a `jev:finding`, `jev:tier` or `jev:suite-red` row of `events.jsonl`, mirrored to the task's or the run's kata issue like a `driver:` row. Nothing reads the rows: no verdict, route, tier or adoption changes for the same tree. The rollback is deleting the three appends (and the seam they ride on, if wanted); no default flips on this plan, and the reading it opens is owed at `n=5 runs` (`20 tasks` for the per-task rows), the floor of `CLAUDE.md`'s Test doctrine. One more task lands the two-line amendment the #1096 comment scheduled for `CLAUDE.md`'s no-metered-API gotcha.
**Closes:** #1096

**Tech Stack:** Node ESM (`fleet/run-engine.mjs`, `fleet/run-main.mjs`, a new `fleet/jev-client.mjs` over the platform `fetch`, the engine sims under `fleet/tests/test_run_engine_*.mjs` on the rig `fleet/tests/_engine_helpers.mjs`, bridged by `tests/test_fleet_suite.py`), bash (`fleet/sandbox-boot.sh`), the contract `fleet/CONTRACT.md`, the developer doc `CLAUDE.md`. No SDK and no new dependency: one `POST` with a JSON body.

**Spec:** none — #1096 and its two comments are the signed input, restated in each task's Context.

**Parallelization rationale:** two waves. Wave 1 has width 2: Task 1 (the client, the engine's `jev` seam, the boot's hostname, the contract and the rig) and Task 5 (the `CLAUDE.md` amendment) share no file and no symbol. Wave 2 has width 3: Tasks 2, 3 and 4 each append one row at one site of `fleet/run-engine.mjs` and each names its own sim; their same-file edits fold, and their sites are 1,300 to 2,000 lines apart, so no two insert at one location. The chain wave 1 → wave 2 is justified by runtime behaviour, not shape: each row task's sim drives the engine through the `jev` argument and the `fakeHub` that Task 1 adds to the rig, and asserts its row reached the hub through the `jev:` routing Task 1 puts in `kataUidFor` — a sim cannot run against a seam that does not exist yet. Task 5 shares nothing with anyone.
