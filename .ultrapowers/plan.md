# Publish-as-probe: a TinyApp run deploys itself and checks itself live

**Grammar:** claims-v1
**Claim:** do: launch a TinyApp plan that names its deploy and its live probe; see: after the run merges, the app is on its production Durable Object, the probe ran against the live URL, and the run's record says published true or false with the probe's exit. (elicited)
**Summary:** Today a TinyApp run ends at a merged pull request and deploying is your hand. This gives a plan two lines, what deploys it and what checks it live, and the sandbox runs both after its own merge, rolls the deploy back when the check is red, and writes the outcome on the run's tag. After it, a green run is a deployed app you can open, and a red check is a rollback you can read, with nothing to do by hand but the one-time token.
**Goal:** three plan header lines — `**Publish:**`, `**Verify:**`, `**Rollback:**` — read by `plan_parse.py` and checked by `plan_check.py`; a publish move in `factory/boot.sh` after the self-merge that runs the deploy through the `cloudflare` edge integration, runs the verify probe with `ULTRA_PUBLISH_URL`, rolls back on red, and writes `publish.json` and three `publish:*` rows on the evidence; a ninth doctor row `cloudflare` with its `first-run.md` section, and a launch refusal for a publishing plan whose fleet has no `cloudflare` integration (#835; the fixture-side probe and the first live measurement are the next plan, on popmechanic/tinyapp-fixture).
**Closes:** #835
**Tech Stack:** bash (`factory/boot.sh`), Node 22 ESM (`factory/record.mjs`, `fleet/doctor.mjs`, `fleet/launch.mjs`, `fleet/tests/*.mjs` sims on `node:test`-free `assert`), Python 3 (`plan_parse.py`, `plan_check.py`, pytest under `tests/`).
**Spec:** #835 and its two 2026-09-23 audit comments are the spec; `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-09-tinyapp-state-exams.md` §6 names the move and builds nothing.
