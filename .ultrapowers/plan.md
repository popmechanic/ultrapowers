# exe.dev lobby facts are re-measured by one probe and kept in one list

**Grammar:** claims-v1

**Claim:** After this run I can re-read every exe.dev lobby fact the fleet has paid for with one hand-run command that creates its own throwaway VMs and removes them, prints one line per fact stamped with the lobby's own fingerprint and the date, exits 0 when every fact holds and 1 on any drift; the contract carries the facts as one list a reader cites instead of restating; and the doctor's verb-drift row names the probe when it sees a flag move. It refuses to run while any fleet run's VM is listed. (elicited)
**Summary:** This gives the fleet one command that re-measures what it knows about exe.dev's lobby, the way the kata probe already does for the hub. It exists because every lobby fact in the runbook's Traps was learned from a run that died on it, the doctor only watches a verb's flags, and Shelley confirmed on 2026-09-23 that exe.dev exposes no version, no changelog and no notice when a verb's behaviour changes, so our probe is the sensor. After it a lobby change is a drift line on the laptop before it is a dead run, and the contract holds the facts in one dated list that Traps and issues point at.

**Goal:** #1014 in the shape its body names, with the two prerequisites settled on 2026-09-23: the operator's allowlist is the throwaway-VM set (`new` of a 1-vCPU throwaway with no `--tag`, `comment`, `cp` with and without `--copy-tags=false`, `tag` and `tag -d` on the throwaways only, `share port` on the throwaway, `rm` of every throwaway, plus the read-only verbs; nothing on `claude-max`, `kata`, `kata-hub`, any `gh-*` integration, `integrations edit`/`policy set`, `defaults write`; never while a `fleet-r*` VM is listed), and Shelley's answer (conversation `cKAZXHW` on fleet-counsel): no lobby version marker, no behaviour changelog, no notification channel — stamp each line with the UTC date and the sha256 of `help all --json`, and treat our probe as the sensor. Two tasks: the probe with its sim and index entry; the documents and the doctor's one sentence. Confidence run 5 of cut three, the last before the 0.3.34 bump.
