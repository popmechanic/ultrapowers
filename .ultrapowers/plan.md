# The census joins each supervisor tick to how its worker ended, and a worker the gateway kills before its first turn is sent once more

**Grammar:** claims-v1

**Claim:** After a run, the operator can read every supervisor tick beside how that worker actually ended — its wall, its error, the task's exam exit and whether it folded — and a per-role table of alarms against late or errored workers; and a worker the gateway kills before its first turn is sent once more after a minute, with a reviewer that died named as dead on the task's landing instead of counted as a clean review. (elicited)
**Summary:** This gives the run record two things it could not say before: whether the supervisor's alarms were right, and whether a reviewer the gateway killed was ever a reviewer at all. It exists because over nine runs (204–212, n=9 runs, 42 ticks) the two supervisor readings disagreed on 23 of 42 ticks and nothing joined either to how the worker ended, and because on one bad night five reviewers died on gateway errors (n=5 dispatches over 4 runs, 209–212) and each was counted as a clean review with zero findings. After this run the census prints the join and the per-role table so the arming decision is arithmetic, and a worker that dies before its first turn gets one more try after a minute — an experiment whose rollback is the backoff cell set to 0, no retry — while a reviewer that died is named as dead on the landing rather than scored as clean.

**Goal:** #1218: `skills/ultrapowers/scripts/jev_census.py` gains `--ticks` (one row per supervisor reading joined to its worker's `dispatch:end` and its task's `landing`) and `--outcomes` (the per-role, per-reading table of fired ticks against late or errored workers). #1219: a pure `factory/retry.mjs` (`shouldRetry`, `retrying`), both dispatch sites of `factory/engine.mjs` wrapped by it, a `dispatch.infra_backoff_ms` policy cell, and a referee whose dispatch ended in an error recorded as `died` rather than as zero findings.
**Closes:** #1218 #1219

Spec: none on disk — issues #1218 and #1219 are the brief, and everything a worker needs is in its Context. The sandbox holds no spec.
