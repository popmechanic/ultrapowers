# The supervisor's observed question weighs elapsed time before it can arm

**Grammar:** claims-v1

**Claim:** The observed question's context says a worker under `supervisor.observed.min_elapsed_ms` (policy, default 120000) is reading, not stuck, and `observed` carries `elapsed_ms` as a named fact the question reads; a tick under the floor asks nothing and writes `supervisor:observed` with `skipped: 'under floor'`. (quoted from #1212)
**Summary:** This gives the supervisor's second reading — the one over what a worker actually did — a floor of two minutes before it is asked at all, and tells the question how long the worker has been running so it can weigh that. It exists because that reading called 19 of 27 healthy workers stuck at about ten seconds in, every one of them still reading its task, and neither supervisor reading can be armed on a signal that fires on a worker that has not started (n=4 runs, runs 204–207, 27 ticks, 2026-09-21). After it the reading waits until a worker has run past two minutes and is asked once then, a worker that finishes inside those two minutes leaves a row saying its reading was skipped and why, the rows that remain are the ones worth reading over the next five runs, and nothing acts on either reading meanwhile; the floor ships as an experiment whose rollback is one number in the policy file, `0`, which is today's shape.

**Goal:** #1212: `factory/watch.mjs` gains a per-dispatch observed watch with a floor — held under `minElapsedMs`, asked once at the first assistant turn past it, and a worker that ends under it leaves one `supervisor:observed` row carrying `skipped: 'under floor' and its `elapsed_ms` (the operator's pick of 2026-09-21: defer, not skip); the floor is one cell of `factory/policy.json`, `supervisor.observed.min_elapsed_ms`, value `120000`, `experiment`, rollback `0`; the `supervisor` set's `stuck` question in `factory/questions.json` names `elapsed_ms` in its context and `observed.elapsed_ms` in the set's state; the engine hands the cell to the tick. The pre-registered reading — share of ticks in band after the floor, over 5 runs — is read off the `supervisor:observed` rows this writes (asked against `skipped`); no census script is added, and the arming decision on #1128 follows that reading.
**Closes:** #1212

**Tech Stack:** Node 24 ESM for the factory and its exam, no new npm dependency; JSON for the policy and question documents.
Spec: none on disk — issue #1212 and #1128's decision comment of 2026-09-21 are the brief, and everything a worker needs is in its task's Context. The sandbox holds no spec.
