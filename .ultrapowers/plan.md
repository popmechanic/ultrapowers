# Three record-only Jev rows on the merged seam: a second opinion beside every blocking finding, a difficulty read beside the tier, and whose-red-is-it on every red suite

**Grammar:** claims-v1

**Claim:** Each is one `POST /v1/systemone` from the driver, appended to `events.jsonl` under a kind of its own, mirrored to the kata run issue like every driver row, and read by nothing in this ticket. (quoted from #1096)
**Summary:** Three questions about a calibrated second reader cannot be answered from the tags because the input it needs only exists while a run is live. This plan has the driver record one snap judgment beside every blocking finding, every task's tier and every red suite, and act on none of it. After five runs the record says whether a second opinion, a difficulty read or a whose-red-is-it read is worth wiring in; the rollback is deleting the three appends.

**Goal:** The remainder of #1096 as an `experiment`, on the seam PR #1107 merged (run-177, wave 1: the Jev client `fleet/jev-client.mjs`, the engine's `jev` argument and its `jevRow` closure, `kataUidFor` routing `jev:` kinds by the `driver:` rule, the rig's `jev` passthrough and `fakeHub`, the boot's `TYPESAFE_BASE_URL`, the contract's three-kind paragraph and the README index lines): the driver puts one question set to Jev at three sites of `fleet/run-engine.mjs` — beside every reviewer-returned blocking finding, beside every task's tier at dispatch and at review, and in the unattributed-red branch of a fold — and appends what came back as a `jev:finding`, `jev:tier` or `jev:suite-red` row of `events.jsonl`, mirrored to the task's or the run's kata issue. Run-177's wave 2 lost these three tasks to an infra death (three implementers returned null minutes after another session rotated the Claude bearer), not to the plan; this plan is those three tasks again, against the merged seam. Nothing reads the rows: no verdict, route, tier or adoption changes for the same tree. The rollback is deleting the three appends; no default flips on this plan, and the reading it opens is owed at `n=5 runs` (`20 tasks` for the per-task rows), the floor of `CLAUDE.md`'s Test doctrine.
**Closes:** #1096

**Tech Stack:** Node ESM (`fleet/run-engine.mjs`, the merged `fleet/jev-client.mjs`, the engine sims under `fleet/tests/test_run_engine_*.mjs` on the rig `fleet/tests/_engine_helpers.mjs`, bridged by `tests/test_fleet_suite.py`). No SDK and no new dependency.

**Spec:** none — #1096 and its two comments are the signed input, restated in each task's Context; the seam's contract is `fleet/CONTRACT.md`'s Jev paragraph (lines 241–252 at BASE).

**Parallelization rationale:** one wave of width 3. Each task appends one row at one site of `fleet/run-engine.mjs` — the review-round dedup at line 4458, `runTaskInner`'s `economics` at 3098 plus the reviewer dispatch at 4440, and the unattributed-red branch at 5141 — and names its own guarded sim; the same-file edits fold, and the sites are hundreds of lines apart, so no two insert at one location. Every symbol a task consumes (`makeJevClient`, `fakeHub`, `rig`, `jevRow`) is on main at BASE, produced by no sibling, so there is no chain. A concurrent plan (#1095) edits the same file at the kata attention poll, the hub mirror and the amendment row — none of them one of these three sites — and that overlap folds at publish.
