# Every question the author asks carries a "Please explain" option, and the record counts how often it was taken

**Grammar:** claims-v1

**Claim:** After this run every question I am asked while a plan is authored — the Claim, the execute choice, any sitting-level pick — carries a Please explain option beside the choices. Picking it brings the same question back with the explanation written into it: first in plain words, what I will see or do differently, and what each option costs me; a second time as a concrete before-and-after; a third time the author rewrites the sentence simpler and offers the rewrite as an option. The record counts the rounds, so a release can tell assent from understanding and a sentence that took two or more is one to rewrite. (elicited)
**Summary:** This puts an explain-first path on every question the author asks you. It exists because a first-pass Claim in the author's register is, most of the time, a chain of terms you cannot parse, and a signing ceremony over a sentence you cannot parse is a rubber stamp; asking for the explanation has to cost nothing and count for something. After it Please explain is a listed option on every question, the explanation comes back inside the question itself and gets more concrete each time you ask, and the record's explain count reads beside the Recommended pick rate at each release.

**Goal:** #526, in the shape its 2026-09-16 comment settled on and the operator's brainstorm of 2026-09-22 sharpened: an explain-first option on every operator-facing question of a sitting; on that pick the question is re-asked in place with the explanation written into its own text, escalating by round (plain words and costs, then a before-and-after example, then a simpler rewrite of the sentence offered as an option); and the rounds recorded on the question's register row so the Recommended pick rate can distinguish assent from understanding, and a question that took two or more rounds names a sentence to rewrite at the release. The mechanism form of #239's rule (explain, don't state). Scope: ultrawrite's question ceremony (§Elicit the claim, the execute question, every sitting-level AskUserQuestion), the authoring record (`explain_rounds` per question), the `AUTHORING fact:` line, the census (one column, one totals field) and CLAUDE.md's two doctrine bullets. The ultrapowers skill's setup questions ("Done in the browser?") are yes-or-wait questions and are left as they are. Confidence run 3 of cut three.
